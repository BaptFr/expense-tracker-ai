WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED
---------------------
A new, read-only "Top Categories" insights page at the route /top-categories.
It ranks all expense categories present in the data by total amount spent
(descending), showing for each: rank, a color dot matching the category's
existing chart color (CATEGORY_META), category name, total spent
(formatCurrency), a proportional progress bar, transaction count, and
percentage share of overall spend. Handles the zero-expenses case with the
existing EmptyState component. No filtering, sorting controls, export, or
editing was added — kept intentionally simple per the task scope.
